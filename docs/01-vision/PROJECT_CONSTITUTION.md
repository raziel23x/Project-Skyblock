# PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md

> This document defines the immutable design principles of Project Skyblock.
> These are not implementation details. They are the standards every feature,
> mechanic, document, and contribution should be evaluated against.

---

# Article I — The Mission

Project Skyblock exists to let players build a thriving civilization from an
empty world by combining survival, science, engineering, and creativity while
preserving the spirit of vanilla Minecraft.

---

# Article II — The Laws

## Law 1 — Conservation

Nothing comes from nothing.

Every renewable resource must have a believable source, feedstock, or process.

Questions:
- Where does it originate?
- Why is it renewable?
- What limits it?

---

## Law 2 — Discovery Before Automation

Players should understand a process before they automate it.

Progression:
Manual → Assisted → Mechanical → Industrial → Automated

Automation rewards knowledge rather than replacing it.

---

## Law 3 — Complement Vanilla

Vanilla mechanics are the baseline.

Project Skyblock should extend, enrich, or organize them—not invalidate them.

Examples:
- Iron farms remain useful.
- Villager trading remains useful.
- Redstone remains useful.
- Vanilla farming remains useful.

---

## Law 4 — Purpose

Every feature answers:

Why does this exist?

If no meaningful answer exists, the feature should be redesigned or removed.

---

## Law 5 — Scale, Not Magic

Technology increases:
- throughput,
- efficiency,
- precision,
- sustainability,
- automation.

Technology should not create arbitrary matter or skip entire branches of progression.

---

## Law 6 — Knowledge

Knowledge is progression.

Research, experimentation, observation, and understanding unlock capability.

---

## Law 7 — Renewable Through Understanding

Scarcity is acceptable early.

Long-term sustainability should come from learning how systems work.

---

## Law 8 — Civilization

Every mechanic should move the player toward building a functioning civilization.

The end goal is not collecting machines.

The end goal is creating a world worth living in.

---

## Law 9 — Creativity

Whenever practical, systems should provide multiple valid solutions.

Avoid a single mandatory design for factories, farms, or infrastructure.

---

## Law 10 — Data First

Gameplay content should be data-driven whenever practical.

Java provides frameworks.
Data defines content.
Configuration enables customization.

---

# Design Review Checklist

Before approving any new feature ask:

- Does it obey the Constitution?
- Does it fit the Four Ages?
- Does it complement vanilla?
- Does it strengthen the civilization narrative?
- Is there a simpler design?
- Is it extensible?
- Can it be documented clearly?

If the answer to several of these questions is "no", revisit the design.

---

# Contributor Pledge

Every contributor should strive to leave the project more coherent than they found it.

New ideas are encouraged.

Unnecessary complexity is not.

The goal is not to build the largest skyblock mod.

The goal is to build the most cohesive one.
