# CIVILIZATION_BLUEPRINT.md

> "Project Skyblock is not about surviving on a tiny island.
> It is about proving that knowledge can transform nothing into a civilization."

---

# The Core Idea

Every mechanic, every machine, every process, and every resource should answer a single question:

**How does this help a civilization exist?**

The player is not climbing a tech tree.

The player is rebuilding the foundations of civilization from almost nothing.

---

# The Four Ages

## Age I — Survival

Question:
> Can I stay alive tomorrow?

Focus:

- Shelter
- Fire
- Water
- Food
- Trees
- Dirt
- Basic tools

Design Goals

- Every resource matters.
- Mistakes are recoverable.
- The player learns observation before automation.

The player should feel:

- Curious
- Careful
- Hopeful

---

## Age II — Settlement

Question:
> Can I stay here forever?

Focus:

- Agriculture
- Forestry
- Ecology
- Storage
- Animal husbandry
- Infrastructure

Design Goals

- Replace scarcity with sustainability.
- Introduce planning instead of reaction.
- Create permanent systems.

The player should begin saying:

"I built a home."

---

## Age III — Industry

Question:
> Can I produce more than I consume?

Focus:

- Metallurgy
- Chemistry
- Machines
- Energy
- Logistics
- Recycling

Design Goals

- Build production chains.
- Reward understanding of systems.
- Scale through engineering instead of grinding.

The player should begin saying:

"I built a factory."

---

## Age IV — Mastery

Question:
> Can I reshape the world?

Focus:

- Dimensional science
- Advanced automation
- Environmental engineering
- Megaprojects
- Scientific research
- Civilization-scale infrastructure

Design Goals

- Remove practical limits without removing meaningful choices.
- Encourage creativity rather than repetition.

The player should say:

"I built a civilization."

---

# The Pillars of Civilization

## Survival

Without survival there is no future.

Supports:

- Biology
- Agriculture
- Forestry
- Ecology

---

## Infrastructure

Infrastructure allows civilization to grow.

Supports:

- Logistics
- Machines
- Energy
- Construction

---

## Knowledge

Knowledge converts survival into progress.

Supports:

- Research
- Chemistry
- Metallurgy
- Dimensional Science

---

## Resources

Resources are never the goal.

Resources exist to enable civilization.

Every resource should answer:

- Why does it exist?
- Where does it come from?
- How does it become renewable?
- Which civilization problem does it solve?

---

# The Technological Revolutions

## Fire

The first technology.

Fire enables:

- Cooking
- Charcoal
- Ceramics
- Glass
- Smelting
- Chemistry

Without fire there is no civilization.

---

## Agriculture

Permanent settlements become possible.

Surplus creates specialization.

Specialization creates progress.

---

## Engineering

Human effort becomes mechanical effort.

Machines multiply labor.

---

## Industry

Production becomes continuous.

Waste becomes input.

Efficiency becomes important.

---

## Science

Observation becomes experimentation.

Experimentation becomes understanding.

Understanding becomes innovation.

---

# Design Test

Every proposed feature should answer YES to these questions.

1. Does it help build a civilization?
2. Does it complement vanilla?
3. Does it make logical sense?
4. Does it reward knowledge?
5. Does it fit one of the Four Ages?
6. Does it encourage creativity?
7. Can players understand why it exists?

If not, redesign it.

---

# The Final Goal

Players should not remember Project Skyblock because it had the best machines.

They should remember it because they looked across a world that was once an empty platform and realized:

"I built forests.

I built farms.

I built factories.

I built power.

I built science.

I built a civilization."

That is the experience Project Skyblock is designed to create.
