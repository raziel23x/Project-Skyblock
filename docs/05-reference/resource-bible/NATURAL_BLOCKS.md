## Dirt

### Identity
The foundation of agriculture and permanent settlement.

### Civilization Role
Without renewable soil, civilization cannot expand beyond its starting platform.

### Vanilla Purpose
Supports crops, grass, trees, flowers, and many natural mechanics.

### Bootstrap Acquisition
- Starting island
- Limited early supply
- Silk Touch grass recovery (late early-game)

### Renewable Method
- Compost
- Biological decomposition
- Soil maturation

### Industrial Production
Biomass processing and automated composting.

### Automation Stage
Settlement → Industry

### Storage
- Early: Chests / Barrels
- Mid: Functional Storage
- Late: Digital storage with Functional Storage as bulk backing

### Related Systems
Biology, Agriculture, Ecology

### Vanilla Compatibility
Vanilla composters remain useful throughout the game.

### Balance Notes
Dirt should become renewable relatively early because it enables player creativity.

## Cobblestone

### Identity
The first industrial construction material.

### Civilization Role
Allows expansion of infrastructure before refined building materials exist.

### Bootstrap Acquisition
Generated from lava + water.

### Renewable Method
Vanilla cobblestone generator.

### Industrial Production
Mechanical generators and processing lines.

### Automation Stage
Survival

### Storage
Functional Storage is the preferred long-term bulk solution.

### Related Systems
Machines, Logistics, Construction

### Vanilla Compatibility
Classic cobblestone generators remain completely valid.

### Balance Notes
Project Skyblock embraces this iconic mechanic instead of replacing it.

## Sand

### Identity
Glass, concrete, filtration, and industrial chemistry feedstock.

### Bootstrap Acquisition
Small starting deposits, dimensional sources, or planned processing.

### Renewable Method
Late-game geological or chemical processing.

### Industrial Production
Industrial aggregate production.

### Automation Stage
Industry

### Balance Notes
Should not require tedious wandering in a void world.
