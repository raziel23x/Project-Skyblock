# Research System

## Purpose

Research records discovery, teaches systems, and structures progression without reducing the game to a conventional quest checklist.

## Core Design Rules

- Research represents understanding, not arbitrary permission.
- Players should perform or observe a process before its advanced automation unlocks.
- Vanilla knowledge should remain useful.
- Documentation should explain systems without spoiling every discovery immediately.

## Research Sources

- Performing experiments
- Producing a material
- Observing an entity or environment
- Analyzing a sample
- Visiting a dimension
- Building a functional apparatus
- Recovering records or artifacts
- Trading knowledge

## Research Outputs

- Journal entries
- Process diagrams
- New recipe visibility
- Automation blueprints
- Diagnostic tools
- Safer or more efficient methods
- Historical and environmental context

## Progression Role

Research should connect systems:

Biology may unlock compost understanding. Chemistry formalizes alchemical processes. Metallurgy depends on heat, mineral preparation, and material testing. Dimensional science depends on energy, advanced materials, and End or Nether observations.

## Vanilla Integration

Advancements may provide milestones and compatibility. The recipe book may remain useful. The research journal should add explanation and system relationships rather than duplicate every recipe.

## Player Agency

Multiple discoveries may lead to the same concept. A player should not be permanently blocked because they missed one exact interaction.

## Restrictions

- Avoid research points gained by idle grinding.
- Avoid giant linear trees with only one valid route.
- Avoid mandatory reading walls.
- Avoid hiding basic survival information needed to avoid irreversible failure.

## Open Questions

- Is research per-player, per-team, or per-world?
- How is multiplayer synchronization handled?
- Should discoveries be shareable through books or data items?
- What information remains hidden until experimentation?

---

## Feature Review Checklist

Before implementation, confirm that the proposed feature:

- complements vanilla Minecraft rather than disabling it;
- has an understandable cause and effect;
- introduces knowledge before automation;
- supports the journey from survival to civilization;
- avoids unnecessary grind and arbitrary recipe chains;
- can be expressed through data where practical;
- has a clear place in progression;
- defines failure, recovery, and renewable paths;
- does not accidentally invalidate several other systems.

## Status

Design specification. Exact values, recipes, machine costs, and timings remain subject to playtesting.
