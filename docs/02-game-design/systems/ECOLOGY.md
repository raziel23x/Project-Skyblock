# Ecology System

## Purpose

Ecology connects isolated biological systems into a functioning environment. It governs habitat creation, population support, biome reconstruction, pollination, decomposition, and the gradual transformation of an artificial platform into a living world.

## Core Design Rules

- Ecology is about relationships, not a single machine.
- Habitats should attract or support life through understandable conditions.
- Vanilla spawning rules should be respected where practical.
- The first specimen can be introduced intentionally; later populations should be renewable.

## Habitat Components

- Space
- Light level
- Temperature
- Water
- Vegetation
- Soil or substrate
- Shelter
- Food sources
- Biome or environmental identity

## Progression

### Basic Habitat

Grass, light, water, and sufficient space allow ordinary passive life to appear or survive.

### Managed Habitat

The player builds dedicated areas for animals, pollinators, fungi, aquatic life, and hostile mobs.

### Reconstructed Ecosystem

Multiple species and resource loops support one another.

### Engineered Biosphere

Late-game systems maintain controlled environments across dimensions.

## Bootstrap Specimens

Potential bootstrap methods include:

- Natural vanilla spawning
- Rescue or migration events
- Trading
- Dimensional discoveries
- Eggs, spores, or preserved samples
- Carefully limited quest or research rewards

The method must fit the fiction and avoid handing the player an entire ecosystem for free.

## Vanilla Integration

- Vanilla mob spawning remains available when valid conditions exist.
- Vanilla breeding remains the primary baseline.
- Mob farms remain valid.
- Project systems improve control, habitat specialization, and ethical or non-combat alternatives.
- Biome-dependent mechanics should receive skyblock-compatible methods without erasing biome identity.

## Automation

Ecology automation should manage environments rather than simply spawn entities from power.

Examples:

- Lighting control
- Feeding
- Population separation
- Egg collection
- Waste recovery
- Habitat climate control

## Restrictions

- No generic machine that creates arbitrary animals from energy alone.
- Avoid hidden ecological requirements that players cannot diagnose.
- Do not force complex simulation on decorative builds.
- Population systems must be performance-conscious.

## Open Questions

- How are biome requirements represented in void dimensions?
- Should habitats create local virtual biome conditions?
- How are rare mobs introduced without relying on nonexistent structures?
- What safeguards prevent entity overpopulation?

---

## Feature Review Checklist

Before implementation, confirm that the proposed feature:

- complements vanilla Minecraft rather than disabling it;
- has an understandable cause and effect;
- introduces knowledge before automation;
- supports the journey from survival to civilization;
- avoids unnecessary grind and arbitrary recipe chains;
- can be expressed through data where practical;
- has a clear place in progression;
- defines failure, recovery, and renewable paths;
- does not accidentally invalidate several other systems.

## Status

Design specification. Exact values, recipes, machine costs, and timings remain subject to playtesting.
