# Alchemy System

## Purpose

Alchemy is the bridge between observation, Minecraft magic, and formal chemistry. It provides early experimentation with heat, dissolution, crystallization, infusion, fermentation, and transformation before industrial scientific equipment exists.

## Identity

Alchemy should feel mysterious but consistent. It is not permission for arbitrary recipe magic.

The player observes repeatable properties and gradually turns folklore into science.

## Progression

### Folk Processes

- Drying
- Steeping
- Fermentation
- Ash and charcoal use
- Primitive solvents
- Herbal mixtures

### Practical Alchemy

- Distillation
- Crystallization
- Calcination
- Infusion
- Precipitation
- Reagent testing

### Transition to Chemistry

Processes become measured, named, and reproducible. Alchemical apparatus evolves into laboratory equipment.

## Inputs

- Plants
- Fungi
- Minerals
- Ash
- Charcoal
- Water
- Alcohols or fermented fluids
- Mob-derived materials
- Nether and End substances

## Outputs

- Reagents
- Extracts
- Salts
- Pigments
- Catalysts
- Potions or potion precursors
- Purified substances

## Vanilla Integration

Vanilla brewing remains fully functional. Alchemy may provide additional ingredient preparation, alternate reagent paths, and deeper explanations for potion materials.

The brewing stand should retain a useful role rather than becoming decorative.

## Automation

Early alchemy is manual and batch-based. Later equipment controls temperature, timing, purity, and fluid transfer.

## Restrictions

- Transformations need a defined principle, catalyst, or Minecraft-magical rule.
- Alchemy should not bypass entire material progressions.
- Avoid a massive list of single-use powders with no shared system.
- Mystery should come from discovery, not undocumented randomness.

## Open Questions

- How much of vanilla potion brewing should be exposed as chemical processing?
- Are magical properties data-driven traits?
- Where is the boundary between alchemy and dimensional science?
- Should failed experiments produce useful byproducts?

---

## Feature Review Checklist

Before implementation, confirm that the proposed feature:

- complements vanilla Minecraft rather than disabling it;
- has an understandable cause and effect;
- introduces knowledge before automation;
- supports the journey from survival to civilization;
- avoids unnecessary grind and arbitrary recipe chains;
- can be expressed through data where practical;
- has a clear place in progression;
- defines failure, recovery, and renewable paths;
- does not accidentally invalidate several other systems.

## Status

Design specification. Exact values, recipes, machine costs, and timings remain subject to playtesting.
