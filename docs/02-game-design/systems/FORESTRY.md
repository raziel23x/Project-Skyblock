# Forestry System

## Purpose

Forestry expands the starting tree into a renewable material economy. It covers tree propagation, wood production, orchards, resin, bark, fibers, charcoal feedstock, and managed woodland.

## Core Design Rules

- The starting tree is valuable and must never be casually invalidated.
- Vanilla saplings and tree growth remain functional.
- Project forestry adds predictability, specialization, and industrial scale.
- Tree products should reflect tree identity where practical.

## Progression

### Survival Forestry

- Protect the first saplings
- Recover sticks, leaves, bark, and wood
- Create primitive charcoal and compost

### Managed Forestry

- Nurseries
- Orchards
- Pruning or growth management
- Resin and sap collection
- Wood sorting

### Mechanical Forestry

- Powered saws
- Log handling
- Bark separation
- Chipping
- Replanting systems

### Industrial Forestry

- Controlled growth chambers
- Large orchards
- Biomass processing
- Specialized timber and chemical feedstocks

## Inputs

- Saplings
- Soil
- Water
- Nutrients
- Light
- Time
- Optional grafts or trait samples

## Outputs

- Logs
- Planks
- Sticks
- Leaves
- Bark
- Resin
- Sap
- Fruit
- Nuts
- Fibers
- Wood chips
- Biomass

## Vanilla Integration

Vanilla tree farms remain valid, including redstone or flying-machine designs. Project systems offer safer replanting, product separation, orchard management, and specialized outputs.

Bonemeal remains useful. Forestry fertilizers may improve reliability or produce secondary products.

## Automation

Automation should progress from manual cutting to mechanical harvesting, then to managed replanting and product routing.

## Restrictions

- Do not make trees behave like instant resource blocks.
- Avoid requiring unique machines for every wood species.
- Do not punish decorative tree planting.
- Trait systems should not make unmodified vanilla trees obsolete.

## Open Questions

- Should grafting exist?
- Should resin tapping damage or slow trees?
- How should giant trees and modded trees be handled?
- Are tree traits stored in sapling NBT/data components or external registries?

## Feature Review Checklist

Before implementation, confirm that the proposed feature:

- complements vanilla Minecraft rather than disabling it;
- has an understandable cause and effect;
- introduces knowledge before automation;
- supports the journey from survival to civilization;
- avoids unnecessary grind and arbitrary recipe chains;
- can be expressed through data where practical;
- has a clear place in progression;
- defines failure, recovery, and renewable paths;
- does not accidentally invalidate several other systems.

## Status

Design specification. Exact values, recipes, machine costs, and timings remain subject to playtesting.
